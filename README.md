## 🩺 Clinical Specialties & Procedures

### 1. Routine & General Dental Care (Common Indian Clinic Procedures)
- **Comprehensive Dental Consultation & Digital X-Rays:** Diagnostic checkups with computerized RVG imaging.
- **Painless Tooth Extractions:** Gentle simple extractions and decayed tooth removal with local anesthesia.
- **Wisdom Tooth Surgery & Impaction Removal:** Surgical extraction for painful, partially erupted, or tilted wisdom teeth.
- **Tooth-Colored Cavity Fillings (Composite / GIC):** 100% mercury-free aesthetic tooth-shade fillings that bond chemically to enamel.
- **Acute Toothache Emergency & Pain Relief:** Fast-action pulpal decompression and sedative dressing for throbbing toothache.
- **Teeth Whitening & Fluorosis Stain Removal:** In-office enamel-safe bleaching removing tea, coffee, and water fluorosis stains.
- **Dental Caps / Crowns & Fixed Bridges:** CAD/CAM precision-milled Zirconia and PFM caps to protect root canal treated teeth.
- **Bleeding Gums, Pyorrhea & Bad Breath Treatment:** Medicated subgingival cleaning and ultrasonic scaling for loose teeth and halitosis.
- **Pediatric Dental Care:** Gentle, fear-free treatments for milk tooth cavities, pulpectomy, and topical fluoride varnish.
- **Denture Adjustment & Repair:** Same-day clinical relining, suction adjustment, and repair for slipping dentures.

### 2. Advanced Oro-Dental Surgery Wing
- **Bloodless Laser Dentistry:** Suture-free soft-tissue surgery with zero bleeding and same-day recovery.
- **Single-Sitting Rotary Endodontics (RCT):** Motorized NiTi instrumentation with digital apex measurement.
- **Single-Visit & Immediate Dental Implants:** Titanium osteointegrated implants restoring chewing strength.
- **Orthodontics & Clear Aligners:** Invisible aligner trays and modern ceramic/metal braces.
- **Ultrasonic Scaling & Airflow Polishing:** Plaque, tartar, and extrinsic tobacco/stain removal without enamel abrasion.
- **Fixed & Flexible Dentures:** Lightweight BPS and flexible partial dentures for comfortable smiles.

### 3. Aesthetic & Laser Dermatology Wing
- **Carbon Laser Peel Facial ("Hollywood Glow"):** Liquid carbon application paired with Q-switched Nd:YAG laser for deep exfoliation and collagen stimulation.
- **Laser Skin Tag, Mole & Warts Excision:** Precision vaporization leaving flawless skin without surgical scarring.
- **Q-Switched Laser Tattoo Removal:** High-intensity nanosecond photomechanical pigment shattering.
- **Permanent Laser Hair Reduction:** Targeted follicular photothermolysis with integrated skin contact cooling.
- **Acne Scar & Melasma Depigmentation:** Fractional laser remodeling and targeted melanin breakdown.
- **Anti-Aging Fractional Laser Resurfacing:** Micro-thermal skin rejuvenation smoothing fine lines and pores.

## ✨ Key Interactive Features

1. **Live Dynamic Clinic Status:**
   - Real-time indicator in the navigation bar computing whether the Simlapal flagship clinic is currently **Open Now** or **Closed** based on local operating hours (6:00 AM – 2:00 PM & 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM).
2. **Smart 3-Step Appointment Stepper:**
   - **Step 1:** Select consultation chamber & specialty treatment (Routine Care, Surgery, or Laser Clinic).
   - **Step 2:** Chamber-restricted date selection (e.g. Panchmura only allows Mondays and Thursdays; Raipur allows Tuesdays and Fridays; Laxmisagar allows Sundays; Simlapal allows all days). Includes quick date pills and morning/afternoon/evening time slots.
   - **Step 3:** Patient name, 10-digit mobile number, age, gender, and medical concern notes.
   - **Step 4:** Instant printable reference slip with a 1-click pre-filled WhatsApp confirmation button sent directly to the clinic hotline (+91 86973 94358).
3. **Interactive Chamber & Schedule Explorer:**
   - Detailed tab switcher for all 4 consultation centers across Bankura.
   - Live embedded Google Map preview for Simlapal Hoi Choi Building, plus photo toggle showing the clinic exterior and surgical operatory.
   - Landmark and transit/parking tips for patients traveling from rural and town areas.
4. **Interactive 3-Wing Service Switcher:**
   - Toggle between **Routine Dental Care**, **Oro-Dental Surgery**, and **Laser & Aesthetic Clinic**.
   - Procedure preview cards with recovery time, comfort index, duration, and clinical modal inspection.
5. **Authentic Photographic Gallery & Patient Reviews:**
   - Uncompressed genuine clinic interior, laser operatory, sterilized dental unit, and patient testimonials.
6. **Floating WhatsApp Hotline & Emergency Banner:**
   - Accessible quick-contact widgets for immediate dental pain triage and consultation bookings.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (version 18 or higher)
- npm or yarn

### Installation & Development
```bash
# 1. Install dependencies
npm install

# 2. Start local development server
npm run dev

# 3. Build for production
npm run build
```
